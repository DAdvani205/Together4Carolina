Your Generosity Changes Lives

Together 4 Carolina is a fund hosted by the North Carolina Community Foundation. When you give, your gift supports our work strengthening underserved communities across North Carolina through healthy food access, healthcare, education, and more.

Ways To Give

One-Time Donation

Every gift, no matter the size, makes a meaningful impact. A one-time donation helps us fund vital programs and prepare to serve the communities that need us most. If donating through a Donor Advised Fund or an Employer Charitable Campaign, please contribute to the North Carolina Community Foundation, and under special instructions specify “Together 4 Carolina Charitable Fund.”

Your time is just as valuable as a financial gift. Volunteering helps us show up for neighbors, strengthen communities, and put our shared values into action.
